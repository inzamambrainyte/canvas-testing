## Detailed Pricing Analysis

### ElevenLabs Pricing (Credit-based)

**Credit System:**
- ~1,000 characters = 1 credit
- ~1 credit = 1 second of audio

**Plans:**
- **Free**: 10,000 credits/month (~10 minutes)
- **Starter ($5/month)**: 30,000 credits (~30 minutes)
- **Creator ($22/month)**: 100,000 credits (~100 minutes)
- **Pro ($99/month)**: 500,000 credits (~500 minutes)

### Cost Comparison (Per Video)

### When to Use ElevenLabs

✅ **Choose ElevenLabs if:**
- You need **premium, human-like quality**
- You want **emotional expression** in voice
- You need **voice cloning** (custom voices)
- You have **low-medium volume** (under 100 videos/month)
- Quality > Cost

❌ **Stick with OpenAI if:**
- You need **cost-effective** solution
- You have **high volume** (100+ videos/month)
- You want **simple setup** (same API key)
- Good quality is sufficient

## Recommendation

**For Your Use Case:**
- **Start with OpenAI TTS** - Cost-effective, good quality, simple
- **Upgrade to ElevenLabs** if:
  - You need premium quality for client work
  - You want voice cloning features
  - You have budget for $22-99/month
  - Quality is more important than cost

**Hybrid Approach:**
- Use OpenAI for bulk generation
- Use ElevenLabs for premium/final videos
- Make it configurable per project
